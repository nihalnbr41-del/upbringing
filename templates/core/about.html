Our Story

Care for the person
behind the parent.

Upbringing is imagined as a gentle, easy-to-use space for mothers navigating pregnancy, birth, and early parenthood.

Why We Exist

Because support should feel within reach.

New motherhood can bring joy, uncertainty, exhaustion, and big changes. We want to make it easier to find reliable information, supportive conversation, and practical essentials.

Our goal is to build a kind and inclusive experience, with clear boundaries between peer support, professional care, and urgent mental-health help.
